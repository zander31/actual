Give the fork its own visual identity, after Robinhood's light theme: a white canvas with hairline structure, one saturated green doing every interactive job, and the figures scaled up as the primary content. The palette is regenerated from upstream's own ramps with each step's OKLCH lightness preserved, so every semantic token keeps its contrast role; the brand green and red are reserved as fills and the deep steps carry text, which fixes several upstream light-theme colours that sat below 4.5:1. Buttons become capsules that press in rather than lifting on a shadow, Instrument Sans replaces Inter throughout, and the Calendar and Forecast pages now lead with the number they exist to report — the Forecast curve changes colour at zero instead of shading a region red.
